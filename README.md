# Cellared for Claude

Bring your Cellared wine collection into Claude. Ask what to open tonight, compare vintages, check drinking windows, and log what you drank, all from your own cellar.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin, or add the Cellared connector from the Claude directory.
2. When Claude asks you to connect, sign in with your Cellared account (or create one) and choose what Claude may read and change.
3. Try: "What from my cellar should I open with steak tonight?"

Manage or revoke access any time at https://cellared.ai/oauth/consent.

## What it can do

- Search the bottles you own and their drinking windows
- Explain why a bottle's window falls where it does
- Read your saved tastings and, on eligible plans, Your Palate
- Record an opened bottle with a rating and notes, correct it, or undo it
- Add bottles and edit quantity or region

It does not buy or sell wine, scan labels, or import files.

## Privacy

Cellared shares only the data you permit, only with the conversation you connect. See https://cellared.ai/privacy for collection, storage, sharing, retention and contact details.
